## 🛠️ Project Covers

| Area | Details |
|---|---|
| 📈 Financial Analysis | 5 years of P&L, Balance Sheet & Cash Flow (FY21–25) |
| 🔮 Forecasting | FY26 & FY27 projections using Assumption-Based Growth Rate & Common Size method |

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📉 Revenue & Margin Trend
- Revenue peaked in **FY22** during the post-COVID global steel boom
- EBITDA margins crashed from **26% → 5%** in FY24 due to rising coal & input costs

### 🔄 Profitability Recovery
- Company reported a **net loss in FY24** — recovered profitably in FY25
- FY25 EBITDA margin recovered to **11.9%** showing operational resilience

### 🏦 Leverage & Capital Structure
- Total Debt at **₹89,000 Cr** — D/E ratio close to 1x, manageable but rising
- Asset base grew from **₹2.44L Cr to ₹2.79L Cr** — consistent business expansion

### 🔮 Forecast (FY26 & FY27)
- Revenue growth projected at **8.71%** based on historical trend
- Margin recovery expected as input costs normalize
